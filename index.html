SAEIF Sistema de Alerta de Incêndios Florestais
v1.0 · LIVE
01Overview

Real-time forest fire alert and monitoring system integrating NASA FIRMS satellite thermal anomalies, IPMA meteorological data, PROCIV civil protection feeds, and EFFIS fire danger forecasts across the Iberian Peninsula.

Alerts are generated by cross-referencing VIIRS hotspots (SNPP + NOAA-20 + NOAA-21) with PROCIV reports, scored 0–100 using structural risk, FWI, wind, and confirmation data.

02Data Sources
NASA FIRMS · VIIRS IPMA · 222 stations fogos.pt · PROCIV EFFIS · Copernicus ESA WorldCover 2021 OSM Nominatim
03Features

Risk score 0–100 combining structural risk (WorldCover), FWI (EFFIS), wind (IPMA), and PROCIV confirmation. EFFIS ranking ≥80th percentile amplifies score ×1.10.

Fire propagation ellipses at 6h and 12h for ALTO/CRÍTICO alerts, oriented by wind direction.

Layer panel with independent toggles: alerts, hotspots, PROCIV occurrences, structural risk, EFFIS FWI (Today / Tomorrow / Day after).

04Technology Stack
FastAPI PostgreSQL 16 · PostGIS Docker · Nginx Leaflet.js Python · rasterio Hetzner VPS
05Developer

EconoStats Economic Intelligence Dashboard
v0.1 · In Development
01Project Overview

EconoStats Portugal is a public dashboard of 62 economic indicators organised in 9 thematic modules, with a unique GIS layer that links macro data to the territory — showing not just that footwear fell −5.6%, but that this means pressure on Felgueiras and Guimarães, with clickable company profiles.

Data from official sources: GEE, INE, Banco de Portugal, Eurostat, IEFP. All comparisons use year-on-year variation to eliminate seasonality.

Ponte Digital Social Support Platform
v1.1 · Fase 1 · Em Produção
01Project Overview

Ponte Digital is a free, public, non-profit web platform that aggregates, georeferences and publishes information about social support resources across the 16 municipalities of the Lisbon District.

Its central purpose is to eliminate the information gap that prevents citizens in economic vulnerability from accessing existing services. Currently 36 verified resources are live, covering alimentação, saúde, emprego, apoio social and educação.

CAL Criticar a Arbitragem Legalmente
v0.9.1 · Technical Brief
01 Project Overview

CAL is a data analytics system that quantifies deviations between expected and observed refereeing decisions in the Portuguese Primeira Liga, covering seasons 2017/18 to 2025/26.

For each referee, season, and decision type, the system calculates binomial Z-scores and a composite SuspicionScore. Covers 2,673 matches across 9 seasons with 98.4% referee coverage.

Important: CAL does not constitute accusation. It identifies statistically anomalous patterns that merit public scrutiny.

Modular pipeline in six independent phases running inside Docker containers on a Hetzner Cloud VPS (Ubuntu 24.04). Data sources: football-data.co.uk (match statistics) and Sofascore API (referee assignments).

Statistical models: xYC expected yellow card model (Brier 0.067), xRC expected red card model (Brier 0.113), xF Poisson regression (MAE 3.3). Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction at 5% applied to all significance tests.
